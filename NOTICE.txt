Granger Browser

Granger Browser is a native C++/Qt desktop application. The packaged browser engine is
Qt WebEngine and is Chromium-based.

The release package contains third-party Qt runtime components, the Tor Expert
Bundle, the PurpleI2P i2pd runtime, and the ISC-licensed quirc QR decoder by Daniel Beer. Their notices and
license texts are copied into the release `licenses` directory by
`scripts/package-release.ps1`.

Qt modules are distributed under the license options stated by the Qt Project
for each module and build. This notice is not a substitute for the applicable
Qt license texts or distribution obligations. Those texts and obligations must
be reviewed and included as required before publishing a binary package.

The Tor Expert Bundle retains the Tor Project and bundled-library license texts
provided in its `docs` directory. Packaging copies those files without replacing
their terms.

The bundled i2pd 2.61.0 Windows x64 and Linux x86_64 runtimes are provided by
the PurpleI2P Project under the BSD-3-Clause license. Their license and pinned
source/checksum records are retained in the package metadata and under
`third_party/i2pd/`.

Search-provider vector marks from Simple Icons are packaged locally under CC0
1.0. Product names and marks remain the property of their respective owners.
The Mojeek icon comes from Mojeek's official logo resources and is used only to
identify that search provider.

EasyList and EasyPrivacy snapshots are attributed to The EasyList authors
(https://easylist.to/) and are used under CC BY-SA 3.0-or-later. Granger Browser
does not claim authorship of those lists. Exact snapshot metadata and hashes
are packaged in `licenses/EasyList-SOURCES.md`.

Optional AdGuard URL Tracking Protection and Russian filter data are fetched
from AdGuard's official update service into user-writable application data and
are licensed under GPL-3.0-only. Their source metadata is documented in
`licenses/CONTENT_FILTER_SOURCES.md`.

No Python runtime is required to start or use the packaged desktop browser.

Granger Browser Pamp Lite is a clean-room native C++ passive analyzer. The full
Pamp source is stored separately under `pamp/` for development and review, but
has no repository-level license grant and is not linked into or packaged with
the browser. Pamp Lite does not copy its Python implementation, templates,
active scanners, branding, or assets. Details are recorded in
`granger/pamp_lite/ATTRIBUTION.md`.

Project-owner-supplied wallpaper, application icon, AI Chat icon, support-page
artwork, and provider marks are inventoried in `docs/UI_ASSET_SOURCES.md`.
Inventory and embedding do not establish redistribution rights. Those rights
must be confirmed, or the affected assets replaced, before public distribution.

No project-wide Granger Browser license is currently granted. See
`DISTRIBUTION.md` for the release prerequisites that remain open.
